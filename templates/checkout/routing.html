Routing policy

Automatic selection first removes any gateway that cannot take the payment: not connected, unusable credentials, outside your amount limits, or missing a phone number Cashfree needs. It then ranks the rest by your preference. Unknown figures rank last. Ties go to the default.

After a gateway definitively rejects an order, automatic mode may try the next one. An uncertain order is always reconciled first and never replaced.
