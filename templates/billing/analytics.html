How to track an ad

Give each ad its own link

Copy a payment link and add these tags before placing it in an ad:

?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=diwali&utm_content=video_a

Use a different utm_content for each creative. Tags are normalized to lowercase. Attribution is saved on new hosted invoices; it does not rewrite existing invoice payment links. For manual invoices, enter source, campaign, and creative in the invoice form.
