Each delivery carries Mandali-Event-Id, Mandali-Event-Type, and Mandali-Signature headers. The signature is an HMAC-SHA256 of the timestamp, a dot, and the raw body, made with the endpoint’s signing secret. Reject deliveries with a wrong signature or a timestamp older than five minutes, and ignore an event ID you have already processed.
